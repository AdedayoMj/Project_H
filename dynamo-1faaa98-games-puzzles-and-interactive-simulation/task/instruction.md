Produce the final variable-rate irrigation prescription for every field season listed in `/app/input/manifest.json`. The locally generated evidence represents projected field boundaries, campaign-specific analysis grids and crop-development curves, soil-map partitions with layered hydraulic properties, per-cell Sentinel-2 observations, daily weather, initial depletion, and spatial irrigation events. `/app/input/specification.md` is the normative definition of the input schemas, geometry rules, daily state transitions, management zones, ordering, numeric semantics, and output schemas.

Write `/app/output/prescription.geojson`, `/app/output/units.csv`, and `/app/output/summary.json`. The GeoJSON must contain the prescribed clipped analysis-unit geometries and all properties in the normative schema. The CSV must contain the corresponding canonical per-unit records. The JSON summary must contain the required field and zone aggregates. Results must cover every listed campaign exactly once and use finite JSON numbers.

Unit identities, grid positions, and zone assignments are exact. Geometry and clipped area must reproduce the prescribed overlay to within $10^{-6}$ square metres, and area fractions to within $10^{-6}$. Water depths are accepted within the larger of $0.01$ mm or $0.1\%$ of the reference magnitude, crop coefficients and stress coefficients within the larger of $0.0001$ or $0.1\%$, and volumes within the larger of $0.01$ cubic metres or $0.1\%$. The relative band accommodates floating-point overlay, summation-order, and serialization effects only; it does not admit a different physical model or state-transition convention.
