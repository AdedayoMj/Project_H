Construct the unique robust store-and-forward downlink plan for every spacecraft mission in `/app/input/manifest.json`. Each mission combines expiring command, science, and engineering packet batches with intermittent solar charging, finite battery and storage, thermal recovery, station visibility, sequence-dependent antenna slew, and four ordered link-capacity scenarios. `/app/input/specification.md` is normative for schemas, transition order, service and overflow priorities, feasibility, the lexicographic robust objective, serialization, and tie-breaking.

Write `/app/output/downlink-plan.json`, `/app/output/downlink-plan.csv`, `/app/output/robustness-certificate.json`, and `/app/output/solver.py`. The plan must expose every slot decision and its shared spacecraft resource state, plus exact per-scenario class losses and deliveries. The CSV must be a lossless flat view of the slot records. The certificate must reproduce the six-component objective prefix, resource extrema, contact count, scenario outcomes, and canonical plan digest.

`solver.py` must be reusable and accept `--input-root INPUT_DIRECTORY --output-root OUTPUT_DIRECTORY`. The verifier executes it on a private schema-compatible mission with changed packet releases/deadlines, solar profile, station pointings, contact capacities/costs, and resource limits. That execution is isolated from verifier files, child processes, and the network.

All decisions, state values, scenario outcomes, objective components, schemas, ordering, and digests are exact integers or exact strings. A nominal-only plan, greedy earliest-contact schedule, independent per-scenario decisions, copied published plan, incorrect transition order, or merely feasible non-optimal schedule does not satisfy the task.
