Build a pump-contingency response frontier for every campaign in `/app/input/manifest.json`. The evidence combines clipped projected field geometry, layered soil surveys, irregular satellite observations, weather, initial water and salt state, spatial applications with source-specific water quality, a nominal pump capacity, and four campaign-specific scarcity policies. `/app/input/specification.md` is normative for the evidence schemas, physical-state semantics, scenario construction, convex allocations, optimality certificate, ordering, and numeric rules.

Write `/app/output/allocation-frontier.geojson`, `/app/output/allocation-frontier.csv`, `/app/output/optimality-certificate.json`, and `/app/output/solver.py`. Each spatial unit must retain its true clipped geometry and terminal water/salinity audit, then expose its request, need decomposition, policy-specific priority, allocated depth, and shortfall at the ordered `critical`, `severe`, `restricted`, `nominal`, and `recovery` frontier points. The CSV is the canonical flat counterpart of the GeoJSON. The certificate must reconcile every scenario budget, allocated and transition volume, weighted shortfall cost, shadow price, active and satisfied counts, and mean service ratio.

`solver.py` must be a reusable Python program, not a wrapper around fixed outputs. The verifier runs `python3 solver.py --input-root INPUT_DIRECTORY --output-root OUTPUT_DIRECTORY` on a schema-compatible private campaign whose geometry is retained while weather, initial states, crop response, water quality, pump capacity, and frontier policies change. It must discover campaigns through the supplied manifest and create the three data products under the supplied output root.

Identities, grid indices, stress counts, scenario labels, activation/satisfaction labels, Boolean binding decisions, and certificate counts are exact. Geometry and clipped area have a `1e-6` square-metre absolute band; area fractions have a `1e-6` absolute band. Water-state, request, allocation, shortfall, and frontier-gain depths use the larger of `0.01` mm or `0.1%`. Accumulated seasonal depths and salt-leaching index use `0.01` absolute only. Coefficients, salinity, need indices, policy priorities, service/robustness ratios, and shadow prices use the larger of `0.0001` or `0.1%`. Volumes and weighted costs use the larger of `0.01` in their documented units or `0.1%`.

Pointwise tolerance is not permission for systematic drift. Within each campaign, every non-geometric per-unit numeric field is also normalized by its acceptance band; the absolute mean normalized residual must be at most `0.05` and RMS at most `0.25`. Use finite float64 values without intermediate rounding. A single nominal prescription, copied published-world table, independent per-unit clipping, reordered scenario policies, or internally inconsistent certificate does not satisfy the task.
